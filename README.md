
# Omowunmi Fatoki
### Medicinal Chemistry | Computational Chemistry | Drug Discovery

Welcome to my research portfolio!

I am an Industrial Chemistry graduate from the Federal University of Technology, Akure, Nigeria, with First-Class Honours. I am currently a Graduate Research Assistant in the Medicinal Chemistry Laboratory at Covenant University, Nigeria.

My research interests lie at the intersection of medicinal chemistry, computational chemistry, cheminformatics, and drug discovery, with a particular focus on developing potential therapeutic agents against malaria and antimicrobial resistance (AMR).

I am interested in integrating computational approaches with experimental chemistry to accelerate molecular discovery, prioritize promising compounds, and make drug discovery more efficient and sustainable.
