# DFT Results and Quantum Chemical Descriptor Analysis

## 1. Introduction

This section presents the results of the Density Functional Theory (DFT) calculations performed on four selected hexahydroquinoline (HHQ) derivatives identified as promising hits against *Plasmodium falciparum* calcium-dependent protein kinase 4 (PfCDPK4).

The electronic properties of the compounds were investigated through frontier molecular orbital analysis. The calculated Highest Occupied Molecular Orbital (HOMO) and Lowest Unoccupied Molecular Orbital (LUMO) energies were used to estimate important quantum chemical descriptors, including the HOMO–LUMO energy gap, ionisation potential, electron affinity, chemical hardness, chemical softness, electronegativity, chemical potential and electrophilicity index.

The results provide insights into the electronic characteristics and relative chemical reactivity of the selected compounds.

## 3. Interpretation of Quantum Chemical Descriptors

The HOMO and LUMO energies of the four selected compounds were used to estimate their quantum chemical descriptors based on Koopmans' approximation. The calculated HOMO–LUMO energy gaps ranged from 2.1152 to 4.0314 eV. Compounds 01, 02 and 03 exhibited relatively lower energy gaps, suggesting greater chemical reactivity compared with Compound 04.

The chemical softness followed the order 02 > 03 > 01 > 04, while chemical hardness followed the reverse trend, 04 > 01 > 03 > 02. This inverse relationship indicates that Compound 02 has the highest predicted electronic softness, whereas Compound 04 exhibits the greatest chemical hardness.

Compound 04 recorded the highest ionisation potential (6.6927 eV), while Compound 02 exhibited the highest electron affinity (3.9307 eV). The electronegativity values further indicated differences in the electron-attracting tendencies of the compounds, with Compound 02 recording the highest value (4.9883 eV).

All four compounds exhibited negative chemical potential values. Compound 02 also recorded the highest electrophilicity index (11.7640 eV), indicating the greatest predicted electrophilic character among the four compounds.

Overall, the quantum chemical descriptors provide insights into the electronic properties, relative stability and predicted chemical reactivity of the selected HHQ derivatives.

## 4. Equations Used for Quantum Chemical Descriptors

The quantum chemical descriptors were estimated from the HOMO and LUMO energy values using the following relationships:

Ionisation potential:

I=-E_HOMO

Electron affinity:

A=-E_LUMO

HOMO–LUMO energy gap:

ΔE=E_LUMO-E_HOMO

Chemical hardness:

η=(I-A)/2

Chemical softness:

S=1/η

Electronegativity:

χ=(I+A)/2

Chemical potential:

μ=-χ

Electrophilicity index:

ω=μ^2/2η
